Our Story

Scoop To My Lou was founded with one simple goal: create a place where families and friends could enjoy homemade ice cream made with care.

Every batch is handcrafted using quality ingredients, creating over forty unique flavors that have become favorites throughout the community.

Whether you're celebrating a birthday, enjoying a summer evening, or stopping by after dinner, we hope every visit leaves you smiling.
